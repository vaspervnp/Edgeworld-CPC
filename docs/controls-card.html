SHIELDRUNNER

For the Amstrad CPC 6128 (or a CPC with 128K). Version 1.3.

Loading

RUN"SHIELD

Leave the disc in: each planet loads from it as you reach it.

The job

Four shield generators stand round the rim of each planet, draining away. Ride the Runner to them, get off and recharge them, and hold the shield until the clock runs out. Three drained at once, and the shield fails. Lose all your energy, and you fall.

The radar

  • Green generator: holding. Flashing: unstable, see to it. Red: drained; enemies pour out of the breach.
  • Two red at once bring the breach carrier: twelve bolts to bring it down.

Tips

  • Jump crawlers when riding; on foot, shoot them.
  • A tracker hangs still just after it bombs: shoot straight up.
  • Throwers stop to lob rocks: walk at them and shoot.
  • Shot enemies sometimes leave an energy cell: touch it.
  • Hits on the Runner cost you little, but three kill it. Whistle for a spare.

Recharging

Stand at a generator and hold ↓: it charges slowly. Press fire while its core flashes white to pump in a big boost; press at the wrong time and it stalls for a second.
